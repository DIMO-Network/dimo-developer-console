# DIMO i18n glossary

Shared terminology for every DIMO app that ships `es`, `ja`, `zh-Hans` or `zh-Hant`: dimo-login, the developer console, dimo.org, fleet-lite, parentos and the login-with-dimo SDK docs. Translators and translate nodes use these terms and report any term that is missing instead of inventing one silently.

**Priority rule: shipped translations win.** The `es` and `ja` columns come from copy that is already live, in this order: dimo-login's Japanese glossary (`dimo-login/docs/i18n.md`), dimo-login `src/i18n/locales/es.ts` and `ja.ts`, then the fleet-lite and parentos `web/xliff/es.xlf` files. When two shipped sources disagree, the earlier one wins and the other variant is recorded in `notes`. Terms with no shipped translation (mostly console vocabulary) are proposals.

- **zh-Hans** follows mainland usage: 登录, 软件, 数据, 账号, 设置, 车辆. Punctuation `，。“”`.
- **zh-Hant** follows Taiwan usage: 登入, 軟體, 資料, 帳號, 設定, 車輛. Punctuation `，。「」`.
- **es** is neutral, Latin American-leaning and uses _tú_, as in dimo-login. (The fleet-lite and parentos xlf files use _usted_.)
- **CJK spacing:** no space between CJK text and Latin letters or digits (`APIキー`, `1年延長`). Confirmed for `ja` (shipped), proposed for `zh` (`API密钥`, `API金鑰`); see open question 2.
- Placeholders such as `{app}` or `{date}` in this table stand for the real `{{param}}` or ICU syntax, which stays byte-identical.
- Japanese copy was written without a native speaker's review, and every zh column is a proposal until James signs it off.

## Open questions for sign-off

Answer "yes" to accept the default, or give a different choice.

1. **zh form of address, 你 or 您.** Default: 您 for the console and dimo.org (B2B, OEM audience) and 你 for dimo-login and the fleet apps (consumer, matching es _tú_). The zh cells that address the user (关于你 / 關於你) come from dimo-login and use 你; console and dimo.org copy uses 您. Alternative: one form everywhere.
2. **Spacing between CJK and Latin letters or digits.** Default: no space in zh (`API密钥`, `API金鑰`), as in shipped ja (`APIキー`). Taiwan practice often adds a space (`API 金鑰`).
3. **share in zh-Hant, 共享 or 共用.** Default: 共享, consistent with zh-Hans. Google Taiwan uses 共用.
4. **passkey in zh-Hant, 密碼金鑰 or 通行密鑰.** Default: 密碼金鑰 (Google/Microsoft Taiwan). Apple Taiwan uses 通行密鑰.
5. **credits.** Default: 点数 / 點數, not 积分 (reads as loyalty points), and "DIMO Credits" stays English as a product name.
6. **configuration in zh-Hant.** 設定檔 reads as "config file or profile" in Taiwan. Default: 組態; the alternative is to keep 設定檔. zh-Hans stays 配置.
7. **ja revoke.** Default: 取り消す for keys, invitations and access, never for stopping a vehicle share (that is 共有を停止).
8. **All other zh cells are proposals** with no shipped source. Default: you confirm the table as a whole.

## Do not translate

Keep these exactly as written in every language, including inside translated sentences:

- **DIMO** and DIMO product names: Login with DIMO, DIMO Driver, DIMO Developer Console, DIMO Credits, DIMO LTE R1, DIMO Ingest Server, DIMO Docs, Fleet Lite, FleetOS, ParentOS, RentalOS, "Powered by DIMO".
- Partner, platform and competitor names: Smartcar, Tesla, AutoPi, Macaron, Google, Apple, GitHub, App Store, Google Play, Turnkey, ZeroDev, Polygon, Base, MotorTrend.
- Vehicle makes, models and names, app names passed in as params, and email addresses.
- Technical acronyms and protocol terms: API, SDK, URI, JWT, VIN, NFT, SACD, GraphQL, OBD, GPS, CSV, PDF, and permission constants such as `NONLOCATION_TELEMETRY`.

Note: `es.ts` and `ja.ts` translate the browser title "Login with DIMO" as a phrase (_Iniciar sesión con DIMO_, _DIMOでログイン_). That is shipped copy and stays; in new copy, Login with DIMO as a product name stays English.
